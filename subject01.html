What lines up, and what doesn't

  • Across the four tracks there is one note every 250 ms, handed out in turn: Gendang, Rebab, Serunai, Gong, then back to Gendang. That matches the 250 ms hop, so the export script writes one note per analysis step and rotates it across tracks.
  • Because of that rotation, a note on the Gong track isn't driven by prefrontal delta, and a Serunai note isn't driven by temporal gamma. The shaded CTM bands show what each instrument would follow under Table 3.2.
  • Every note is D4 or E4, about 0.23 s long, and velocity rises from 62 to 74 over the minute on all four tracks alike. Band power swings far more than that, so velocity isn't following amplitude as Eq. 3.13 would predict.

Reading the chart

  • Each EEG value is stamped at the end of its 2-second window, so the feature lanes start at 2 s (bar 2).
  • The large spikes on Fp1 and Fp2 are eye blinks. They dominate the Gong's delta band; where they exceed the plotted range, the band is capped at the top of its lane.
  • Each lane is scaled to its own 2nd–98th percentile, so compare shapes over time rather than heights between lanes. The tooltip gives the absolute µV².
  • The sound is a mix recorded from Ableton Live with stand-in instruments, not a live link to the EEG.
