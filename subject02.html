What lines up, and what doesn't

  • Across the four tracks there is one note every 250 ms, handed out in turn: Gendang, Rebab, Serunai, Gong, then back to Gendang. That matches the 250 ms hop, so the export script writes one note per analysis step and rotates it across tracks.
  • Because of that rotation, a note on the Gong track isn't driven by prefrontal delta, and a Serunai note isn't driven by temporal gamma. The shaded CTM bands show what each instrument would follow under the CTM table.
  • Pitch changes in blocks shared by all four tracks: C4 only in the first 8 s (bars 1–4), D4 throughout, and E4 only from 26 s (bar 14). Every note is about 0.23 s long.
  • Velocity dips from 62 to 60 around bars 6–11, then rises to 72 on all four tracks alike. It doesn't follow the band power at each note (|ρ| ≤ 0.17 on every track).

Reading the chart

  • Each EEG value is stamped at the end of its 2-second window, so the feature lanes start at 2 s (bar 2).
  • The large spikes on Fp1 and Fp2 are eye blinks, in about half the windows. They dominate the Gong's delta band; where they exceed the plotted range, the band is capped at the top of its lane.
  • Temporal gamma (the Serunai lane) runs about three times Subject 01's level and stays high throughout, which points to muscle activity at T7 and T8 rather than cortical gamma. The map has no muscle gate.
  • Each lane is scaled to its own 2nd–98th percentile, so compare shapes over time rather than heights between lanes. The tooltip gives the absolute µV².
  • The sound is a mix recorded from Ableton Live with stand-in instruments, not a live link to the EEG.
